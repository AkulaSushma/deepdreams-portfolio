# Page Topology — marry-ai.preview.emergentagent.com

Target URL: https://marry-ai.preview.emergentagent.com/

## Overview & Architecture
- **Framework & Layout**: Single-page application with fixed glassmorphism navigation header, hero section with phone mockup preview, feature cards grid, 4-ceremony interactive cards, template showcase, testimonials carousel, and sticky WhatsApp RSVP action sheet.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Background: Ivory / Warm Cream (`#FBF6EC`, `#FCF9F3`, `#FFFDF9`)
  - Primary Accent: Deep Royal Maroon (`#7E252C`, `#7A1F1B`)
  - Gold Details: Imperial Gold (`#C8A253`, `#D4AF37`, `#F2D98A`)
  - Text Primary: Dark Maroon Chocolate (`#4A2C22`, `#3A1010`)
- **Typography**:
  - Display & Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display`
  - Script Accents: `Great Vibes`
  - UI Text: `Inter`

## Section Breakdown
1. **Header / Navbar (`#navbar`)**:
   - Fixed header with glassmorphism blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
   - Brand logo with heart medallion icon.
   - Quick navigation links (`#features`, `#festivities`, `#templates`, `#testimonials`).
   - Primary CTA: `Create yours`.

2. **Hero Section (`#hero`)**:
   - Left side: Badge `✨ AI-CRAFTED WEDDING INVITATIONS`, title `"Your wedding, worth framing."`, subtext, and dual CTAs (`Craft with AI — free`, `See a live demo`).
   - Right side: Interactive phone container displaying wax seal (`S`), `TAP TO OPEN`, and sliding door panels.

3. **Features Grid (`#features`)**:
   - 6 Feature cards detailing AI love-story writer, cinematic reveals, WhatsApp RSVPs, 5-minute setup, 4-day festivities, and Team Bride vs Groom confetti.

4. **Interactive Festivities Grid (`#festivities`)**:
   - Haldi (Turmeric scratch-off rub canvas).
   - Mehndi (Henna heart trace gesture canvas).
   - Sangeet (Dhol tap with Web Audio synthesized beats).
   - Subha Muhurtham (Glowing Diya lighting canvas).

5. **Template Showcase (`#templates`)**:
   - Theme cards: Royal Maroon, Golden Haldi, Mehndi Blush, Sangeet Nights with live preview triggers.

6. **Love Notes / Testimonials (`#testimonials`)**:
   - Verified couple reviews with avatar medallions, quotes, and location tags.

7. **Footer & Action Sheet (`#cta`)**:
   - Final CTA section with WhatsApp RSVP generator, guest list counter, and `.ics` calendar invitation downloaders.
