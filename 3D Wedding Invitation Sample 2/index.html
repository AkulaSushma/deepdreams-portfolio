Before you ask

Questions, answered

Can I really edit everything myself?

Yes. Names, monogram, muhurat, every function, venue, maps, RSVP link, the secret blessing and the entire colour palette are editable in the browser — no code, no account. Your draft is saved on your own device.

What about the artwork and the films?

The demo uses our own artwork so you can see the real thing immediately. In a paid package we prepare the scrolling artwork and the hidden-moment film from your own pre-wedding photographs, with the same motion and pacing you see here.

Will it work on my relatives' old phones?

It is built phone-first. Image quality, particle count, shadows and effects adapt to each device, and the heavy 3D world downloads only when a guest chooses to enter it.

Is the 3D world included by default?

It's part of the Rajwada package. The other packages end at the finale card; the gateway simply isn't shown.

How do RSVPs reach us?

Paste any Google Form link and it embeds inside the invitation. Leave it blank and the built-in royal RSVP form is used instead.

Can we change details after it's live?

Yes — timings, venues and functions can be updated at any point up to the wedding, and the link your guests already have stays the same.
